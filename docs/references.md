# Suggested references

Books and articles for further study. The notebooks are self-contained and do not
follow any of them; the list is a starting point for readers who want more.

## Asymptotic methods

* G. A. Baker and P. Graves-Morris, *Padé Approximants*, 2nd ed. (Cambridge University Press, 1996).
* G. I. Barenblatt, *Scaling, Self-Similarity, and Intermediate Asymptotics* (Cambridge University Press, 1996).
* C. M. Bender and S. A. Orszag, *Advanced Mathematical Methods for Scientists and Engineers* (McGraw-Hill, 1978; Springer, 1999).
* N. Bleistein and R. A. Handelsman, *Asymptotic Expansions of Integrals* (Holt, Rinehart and Winston, 1975; Dover, 1986).
* N. G. de Bruijn, *Asymptotic Methods in Analysis*, 3rd ed. (North-Holland, 1970; Dover, 1981).
* R. L. Graham, D. E. Knuth and O. Patashnik, *Concrete Mathematics*, 2nd ed. (Addison-Wesley, 1994).
* E. J. Hinch, *Perturbation Methods* (Cambridge University Press, 1991).
* M. H. Holmes, *Introduction to Perturbation Methods*, 2nd ed. (Springer, 2013).
* C. C. Lin and L. A. Segel, *Mathematics Applied to Deterministic Problems in the Natural Sciences* (Macmillan, 1974; SIAM, 1988).
* F. W. J. Olver, *Asymptotics and Special Functions* (Academic Press, 1974; A K Peters, 1997).
* F. W. J. Olver, D. W. Lozier, R. F. Boisvert and C. W. Clark (eds), *NIST Handbook of Mathematical Functions* (Cambridge University Press, 2010), online as the Digital Library of Mathematical Functions (DLMF), https://dlmf.nist.gov.

## Applications

* T. L. Bergman, A. S. Lavine, F. P. Incropera and D. P. DeWitt, *Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer*, 7th ed. (Wiley, 2011).
* M. Born and E. Wolf, *Principles of Optics*, 7th ed. (Cambridge University Press, 1999).
* H. S. Carslaw and J. C. Jaeger, *Conduction of Heat in Solids*, 2nd ed. (Oxford University Press, 1959).
* J. Crank, *The Mathematics of Diffusion*, 2nd ed. (Oxford University Press, 1975).
* C. W. Gardiner, *Stochastic Methods: A Handbook for the Natural and Social Sciences*, 4th ed. (Springer, 2009).
* D. A. McQuarrie, *Statistical Mechanics* (Harper & Row, 1976; University Science Books, 2000).
* H. Risken, *The Fokker–Planck Equation*, 2nd ed. (Springer, 1989).
* N. G. van Kampen, *Stochastic Processes in Physics and Chemistry*, 3rd ed. (North-Holland, 2007).

## Original articles

* J. P. Boyd, "The devil's invention: asymptotic, superasymptotic and hyperasymptotic series", *Acta Appl. Math.* **56**, 1 (1999).
* R. M. Corless, G. H. Gonnet, D. E. G. Hare, D. J. Jeffrey and D. E. Knuth, "On the Lambert W function", *Adv. Comput. Math.* **5**, 329 (1996).
* P. Debye, "Näherungsformeln für die Zylinderfunktionen für große Werte des Arguments und unbeschränkt veränderliche Werte des Index", *Math. Ann.* **67**, 535 (1909).
* F. J. Dyson, "Divergence of perturbation theory in quantum electrodynamics", *Phys. Rev.* **85**, 631 (1952).
* P. Hänggi, P. Talkner and M. Borkovec, "Reaction-rate theory: fifty years after Kramers", *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **62**, 251 (1990).
* H. A. Kramers, "Brownian motion in a field of force and the diffusion model of chemical reactions", *Physica* **7**, 284 (1940).
* P. S. Laplace, "Mémoire sur la probabilité des causes par les événements", *Mém. Math. Phys. Acad. R. Sci. Paris (Savants étrangers)* **6**, 621 (1774).
* H. P. Mulholland, "An asymptotic expansion for $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+1)\exp(-\sigma(n+\tfrac12)^2)$", *Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc.* **24**, 280 (1928).
* H. Poincaré, "Sur les intégrales irrégulières des équations linéaires", *Acta Math.* **8**, 295 (1886).
* A. D. Sokal, "An improvement of Watson's theorem on Borel summability", *J. Math. Phys.* **21**, 261 (1980).
