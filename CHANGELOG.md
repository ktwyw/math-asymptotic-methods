# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-13)

First release: 11 executed notebooks on asymptotic expansions of integrals and sums.

- **Part I, Foundations** (Chapters 1–4): what an asymptotic expansion is; scaling and
  the small parameter; order symbols, gauge functions and terms beyond all orders;
  divergent series, optimal truncation, Borel–Padé, Padé, Shanks and Richardson.
- **Part II, Integrals and sums** (Chapters 5–9): integration by parts and Watson's
  lemma; Laplace's method with its first correction; stationary phase; steepest
  descent; Euler–Maclaurin and the Stirling series.
- **Part III, Worked examples**: 10. Kramers' escape rate; 11. transient heat
  conduction at short and long times.
- The `asymp` module (error slopes, optimal truncation, Shanks, Richardson, Padé,
  high-precision references, Laplace's method with its first correction), with 18 tests.
- Suggested references, Jupyter Book 2 configuration (`myst.yml`) and a workflow
  that publishes the book to GitHub Pages.

The asymptotics of differential equations (boundary layers, matching, WKB, multiple
scales) is the subject of a companion book, `math-perturbation-methods`.
